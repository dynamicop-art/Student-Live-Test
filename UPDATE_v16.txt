PLAY, OBSERVE, UNDERSTAND — v16

Student dashboard > "খেলতে খেলতে বিজ্ঞান" opens a child-friendly Science Playground.
Three experiments are available in one dialog, without adding more Home cards:

1. Newton's apple: release the apple, see a playful reaction and idea bulb,
   then answer why it falls. The scene is explicitly labelled a fictionalised story.
2. Arrow angle: change the angle from 15 to 75 degrees and launch with the same speed.
   The trajectory uses v=20 m/s and g=9.8 m/s² with no air resistance and equal
   start/end heights. Compare 30, 45 and 60 degrees, then answer a short question.
3. Hot water: adjust a temperature slider from 20 to 100°C. Water bubbles at 100°C
   in this simplified normal-pressure model. The explanation notes that pressure
   changes the boiling point. A brief adult-help reminder covers real hot water.

Each experiment has friendly Bengali dialogue, observe-before-quiz, retry hints,
positive feedback and one learning sticker. The three stickers last for this page
session only and do not change any exam score, profile XP or leaderboard.
The existing decorative thermometer pop remains a cartoon effect, not a claim that
thermometers normally burst when water boils.

Existing Space Mission and eight decorative science scenes remain available.
No external AI or network request is needed for the playground itself.
