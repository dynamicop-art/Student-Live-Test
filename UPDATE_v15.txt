COSMIC DASH — INTERACTIVE SPACE MISSION v15

Open the student dashboard and select Launch Mission from Home or the utility bar.
This opens a full-screen game on mobile, or a large space-game dialog on desktop.

- Three difficulty modes: Moon (add/subtract), Orbit (multiply), Warp (mixed).
- Eight questions per mission, three asteroid answer choices each.
- Tap/click an answer or use keys 1, 2, 3. Correct answers fire a visual laser,
  with a particle burst, points and consecutive-answer combo bonuses.
- Move the pointer or drag over the open space background to steer the rocket.
  Steering is decorative: answer selection determines scoring.
- Wrong answers show the correct answer, then you choose when to continue.
- End screen shows score, correct count and best combo; launch another mission.
- No timer pressure, no lives or penalties, and no effect on exam marks/ranks.
- Scores last for this mission only and are not uploaded or saved.
- Close with X, Back to learning or Escape. Keyboard focus returns to the launcher.

Motion pauses while the page is hidden. Device Reduced Motion keeps the playable
questions but stops moving stars, laser/particle effects and animated rocket motion.
Existing background decorative effects pause while the mission is open.
The launcher refuses to open when the inline test runner is active.
